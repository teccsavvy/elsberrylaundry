The Process

How wash & fold works

1

Drop Off Your Bag

Come in during attended hours and hand us your laundry. We'll weigh it and give you a receipt with your estimated total. Tell us if you have any special requests.

2

We Sort & Pre-Treat

We separate your whites, lights, and darks. Any soiled areas get spray-treated before washing. No guessing, no shortcuts.

3

Washed & Dried Properly

We choose the right machine and settings for each load. We don't overload the washers - your clothes get a proper clean.

4

Folded & Packaged

Everything gets neatly folded, sorted and bundled. Easy to put away when you get home.

5

You Get a Text

We'll send you a text when your order is ready. Come pick it up at your convenience within our operating hours.
