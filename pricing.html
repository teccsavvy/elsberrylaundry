How weighing works

We weigh your laundry at drop-off and give you a receipt with the estimated cost. You're charged the final weight after washing. Minimums apply per drop-off, not per pick-up.

Self-Service

Do it yourself, at your pace

Our self-service machines are priced by machine size. You pick the right machine for your load and pay at the machine - card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or coin.

Smaller machines handle regular weekly loads. Our large-capacity machines are ideal for comforters, rugs, and bulky items - one big load beats multiple small ones every time.
