Quick Tips

Getting the most out of your visit

💡

Sort Before You Come

Pre-sort into whites, lights, and darks at home to save time and machine space.

💡

Fill Washers Fully, Not Overstuffed

Load the washer to capacity - but leave room for water and agitation. Clothes that can't move don't get clean.

💡

Fill Dryers Halfway Only

Dryers need airflow to work. Half-full is the right amount for even drying.

💡

Use Less Soap Than You Think

High-efficiency machines need less detergent than you expect. Ask our attendant if you're unsure - they're happy to help.

💡

Big Items? Use the Big Machine

Comforters, rugs, and car seat covers do much better in our large-capacity washers. It's faster and cheaper than running two standard loads.
